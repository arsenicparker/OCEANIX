About OCEANIX & Public Limitations

Scientific Background & Purpose

OCEANIX is an educational decision-support engineering prototype designed to evaluate the practical feasibility of using real marine environmental data to estimate fishing-zone suitability.

Data Sources & Provenance

  • NOAA / NCEI Accession 0306675: 15 years (2005–2019) of Western Indian Ocean Skipjack tuna catch records compiled by the Indian Ocean Tuna Commission (IOTC) and paired with 12 Copernicus Marine physical/biogeochemical ocean variables.
  • INCOIS PFZ Advisories: Operational Potential Fishing Zone advisories issued by the Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services (Ministry of Earth Sciences, GoI) for the Karnataka coastal sector.

Rigorous Evaluation Protocol

The machine learning model was trained strictly on data from 2005 to 2015 and evaluated on an unseen, frozen 2016 to 2019 holdout partition. The Random Forest ensemble outperforms the dummy baseline across MAE, RMSE, and $R^2$.

Ethical Guardrails & Disclaimers

  • Not a Guarantee of Catch: Environmental suitability estimates reflect statistical similarity to historical commercial catch conditions; they do not guarantee fish presence.
  • Not a Navigational Tool: OCEANIX does not assess maritime hazards, waves, cyclones, or sea-state safety. Fishers must always consult official IMD/INCOIS forecasts.
  • Geographic Boundary: The model is calibrated for the Western Indian Ocean pelagic fishery and cannot be extrapolated to nearshore artisanal fisheries.
