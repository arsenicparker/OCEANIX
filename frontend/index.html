ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About OCEANIX

Marine intelligence & decision support · educational prototype

OCEANIX

Explore source-backed marine advisories and datasets.

HOW IT WORKS

Official data, clearly labelled

This version reads a dated set of INCOIS Potential Fishing Zone advisory exports, validates their coordinates, and displays advisory points on a map. The concentration layer shades clusters of those points.

DATA SOURCE

INCOIS products

Advisory locations come from the official INCOIS PFZ export. Ocean Conditions queries INCOIS OSF model forecast fields by selected advisory coordinates and shows the dated ABIS chlorophyll-a satellite map.

Open official INCOIS source ↗

LIMITATIONS & RESPONSIBLE USE

What this prototype does not tell you

  • The concentration heat layer is not a catch probability, fish-count estimate, or OCEANIX prediction.
  • An advisory does not guarantee catch, and an area without a listed advisory does not mean fish are absent.
  • The local PFZ snapshot is dated and does not automatically update. Check the official source for a newer advisory.
  • OSF environmental values are forecasts; ABIS is a regional satellite map, not a selected-pixel reading. Products can be delayed or unavailable.
  • This app is not a navigation or sea-safety tool. Follow official safety guidance.
